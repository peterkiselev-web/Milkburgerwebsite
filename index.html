Three ways to tell the Milk Burger story

Each concept carries the same content: the full menu, Jorge Paredes, Alina and Dima's story, the two rooms, the press, and everything you need to walk in. What changes is how it feels.

  1. 01

    The Griddle

    Cinematic. Scroll and a burger is cooked in front of you, from aged beef on stainless steel to a glass of Billecart-Salmon. Built for the Higgsfield film.

    Open concept
  2. 02

    Cream

    Minimal. Cream paper, burgundy ink, the logo up front, like the Instagram grid and the printed burger papers. The fastest to load and the easiest to keep up to date.

    Open concept
  3. 03

    Smash vs Juicy

    Playful. Alina is team smash, Dima is team juicy. Pick a side and the whole site re-themes around the tiled room or the light room.

    Open concept
