# Questions for the owners

Everything the draft site can't answer from public sources. Anything marked TBC on the site comes from this list.

## Must have before launch
1. **Logo files.** Vector logo (SVG, AI or PDF) and any brand fonts or colour references from the designer.
2. **Full menu with prices.** Exact dish names, descriptions in the kitchen's own words, prices, and allergen information for every dish. A customer's photo of the printed menu on Google Maps (November 2026) lists: Classic Smash 14, Spicy Smash 14, Baconnaise 18, Bone Marrow & Truffle 18, Turkey Burger 16, Nacho Burger 18; Chicken Sando 16, Cuban Sandwich 16; Caesar Salad 9, Cobb Salad 13; Fried Camembert 11, Vegetable Tempura 9, Crispy Chicken Bites 11, Koffmann's Fries 4.5; Fried Cherry Pie & Soft Serve 8, Warm Doughnuts & Soft Serve 6, Soft Serve 5. Is that current? The draft menu still uses press names (The Mexican, Katsu Sando, Chicken Nuggets).
3. **Drinks list with prices,** including the full Champagne list.
4. **Public contact details.** Is 07754 667 939 (listed on SquareMeal) meant to be public? Is there an email for enquiries and press?
5. **Photography.** Full-resolution originals of the press images, plus permission to use the team photo. Who shot them, and do they want a credit? The Cream draft also uses six customer photos from Google Maps reviews (files starting `review-` in `public/images`). The customers own those: before launch, either get each one's permission or replace them with the owners' own shots of the same dishes, the tiled room and the bar. Their own Google uploads (shopfront, team) would also help: they couldn't be downloaded from here.
6. **Chef bio sign-off.** Jorge's title at OMA (head chef or executive chef) and his approval of the wording.
7. **Founders' story sign-off.** How much of the Kyiv story to tell, and whether to mention why they moved to London.
8. **Domain.** Do they own milkburger.uk (matching the Instagram handle) or another domain?

## Good to have
9. Where does the name "Milk" come from?
10. Children, vegetarian and vegan options, gluten-free options.
11. Groups and private hire: possible? How do people ask?
12. Takeaway or delivery: any plans?
13. Accessibility: step-free entrance, accessible toilet.
14. Dogs: welcome or not?
15. Payment: card only? Service charge?
16. Holiday hours: Christmas, Boxing Day, New Year's Eve.
17. Gift cards, merch (the matchboxes?), newsletter sign-up?
18. Are they happy to name suppliers (Mr Txuleta, Billecart-Salmon) on the site?
19. Footage: could the kitchen shoot three reference photos for the scroll film? Raw beef on their griddle, their smash press, their buns.
20. Newsletter: do they want one? Which tool (Mailchimp, Klaviyo or similar), who writes it, and how often?
21. Contact form: which email address should enquiries go to? Do they take private hire and large groups at all?
22. Privacy policy: needed before any form collects real emails (UK GDPR).
23. Fonts: the files or names of the fonts used on their printed menus, so the website body text can match.
