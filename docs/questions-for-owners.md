# Questions for the owners

Everything the draft site can't answer from public sources. Anything marked TBC on the site comes from this list.

## Must have before launch
1. **Logo files.** Vector logo (SVG, AI or PDF) and any brand fonts or colour references from the designer.
2. **Full menu with prices.** Exact dish names, descriptions in the kitchen's own words, prices, and allergen information for every dish.
3. **Drinks list with prices,** including the full Champagne list.
4. **Public contact details.** Is 07754 667 939 (listed on SquareMeal) meant to be public? Is there an email for enquiries and press?
5. **Photography.** Full-resolution originals of the press images, plus permission to use the team photo. Who shot them, and do they want a credit?
6. **Chef bio sign-off.** Jorge's title at OMA (head chef or executive chef) and his approval of the wording.
7. **Founders' story sign-off.** How much of the Kyiv story to tell, and whether to mention why they moved to London.
8. **Domain.** Do they own milkburger.uk (matching the Instagram handle) or another domain?

## Good to have
9. Where does the name "Milk" come from?
10. Children, vegetarian and vegan options, gluten-free options.
11. Groups and private hire: possible? How do people ask?
12. Takeaway or delivery: any plans?
13. Accessibility: step-free entrance, accessible toilet.
14. Dogs: welcome or not?
15. Payment: card only? Service charge?
16. Holiday hours: Christmas, Boxing Day, New Year's Eve.
17. Gift cards, merch (the matchboxes?), newsletter sign-up?
18. Are they happy to name suppliers (Mr Txuleta, Billecart-Salmon) on the site?
19. Footage: could the kitchen shoot three reference photos for the scroll film? Raw beef on their griddle, their smash press, their buns.
