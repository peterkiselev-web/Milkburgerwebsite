- **Single source**: reported by one outlet only. Check with the owners.

## 2. The people

### Alina Kosichkina and Dima Zakhodiakin (founders)
- Business and life partners. Hospitality entrepreneurs from Kyiv who built a portfolio of **32 restaurants in Kyiv**, including the bakery chain **Khlebniy**. (Broadsheet, Hot Dinners 2024, multiple outlets)
- Opened their **first burger restaurant in 2013**. (Kosichkina, via Hospitality & Catering News, SquareMeal)
- Relocated to London after the outbreak of the war in Ukraine. Both live locally in Notting Hill. (SquareMeal, The London Economic, The Nudge)
- Milk Burger is their **first London opening**. A 2024 plan for a Notting Hill bakery called Birdmilk did not go ahead. (Restaurant Online, Hot Dinners)
- Kosichkina's personal Instagram is @kosichkina.
- **Team smash vs team juicy.** Kosichkina: "I'm [in the] smash army and Dima is on [the] juicy, so we have both of them." She expects the juicy burgers to be the bestsellers. (Broadsheet)

### Jorge Paredes (executive chef)
- Executive chef at **Sabor** (Michelin starred) under Nieves Barragán. (The Nudge, SquareMeal, The Caterer)
- Then head chef at **OMA and AGORA** in Borough Market for owner David Carter. OMA opened on 16 April 2024. (The Restaurant Guide, Restaurant Online 2024)
- OMA won its **first Michelin star in under a year** (Michelin Guide GB&I 2025) and became **the UK's first Greek restaurant with a Michelin star**. The Michelin inspector called his cooking "Greek in spirit, and utterly brilliant". (Michelin Guide article, Feb 2025)
- OMA was also named **Michelin Opening of the Year**. (The Nudge, London On The Inside)
- Instagram: @jorge_g_paredes.
- **Title wording:** most sources say "head chef" at OMA; Restaurant Online says "executive chef". Use "head chef" until confirmed.
- **Accuracy note:** several outlets call him "a Michelin-starred chef". The defensible wording for the restaurant's own site is: *"He ran the kitchen at OMA when it won its first Michelin star, after leading the kitchen at Michelin-starred Sabor."*

### David Archer (interior designer)
- Designer behind Chiltern Firehouse, The Standard London, the renewal of Ronnie Scott's, and The Barbary in Notting Hill. Lives in the area. (Broadsheet, SquareMeal)

### Suppliers
- **Beef:** Mr Txuleta, the aged-beef supplier also behind Ibaii steakhouse in the City. (Broadsheet)
- **House Champagne:** Billecart-Salmon. (Broadsheet)

## 4. The space
- Two contrasting rooms, designed by David Archer:
  - An intimate, "chocolate-box" dining room: **burgundy tiles, stainless steel, rust-red banquettes**.
  - A brighter, airier room: **pale blue, soft timber, white-panelled walls**.
- The two-tone idea carries down to the **printed burger papers and matchboxes**. (SquareMeal, The London Economic)
- The site was home to a burger restaurant (Gourmet Burger Kitchen) for **nearly 20 years** before it closed during the pandemic. Kosichkina: "The walls remember all the burger success before." (Broadsheet, Restaurant Online, SquareMeal)
- Shopfront: white-painted facade, black awning with the hand-drawn Milk Burger logo in burgundy. Seasonal window dressing (autumn 2026: pumpkins and dried branches). (Instagram)

## 5. Brand
- **Logo:** hand-drawn, outlined bubble lettering, "MILK BURGER", burgundy on cream. Vector file TBC from the owners.
- **Palette from their own material:** cream, burgundy, black (awning), white (facade), rust (banquettes), stainless steel, pale blue, timber.
- **Voice:** playful and cheeky ("Burgers, but make it sexy") with serious substance underneath (Michelin kitchen, aged beef, Billecart-Salmon).
- **Name origin:** not explained anywhere. Possibly linked to the glossy milk bun, or to their earlier Birdmilk concept (Ptashyne moloko, "bird's milk", is a classic Ukrainian cake). Ask the owners. Do not guess on the site.

## 6. Quotes cleared for use (on record in published press)

**Alina Kosichkina**
- "I take burgers very seriously. We opened our first burger restaurant back in 2013, and right now I feel that burgers and comfort food in general are having a real moment." (Hospitality & Catering News)
- "People are tired of overly complicated food, but at the same time we care more and more about the quality of what we eat." (Restaurant Online, The London Economic)
- "For me, there is nothing better than a burger or sandwich made with the best possible ingredients, paired with great Champagne, wine or a cocktail." (Restaurant Online)
- "Somewhere you can come at any time of the day, without the restaurant closing between lunch and dinner, have something really delicious at a fair price and enjoy it in a beautiful interior. That's exactly the kind of place we wanted Milk Burger to be." (Hospitality & Catering News)
- "The walls remember all the burger success before." (Broadsheet)

**Jorge Paredes**
- "A burger doesn't give you anywhere to hide. The produce, the seasoning, the technique all have to be right." (The London Economic)
- "There's a real discipline in cooking something simple properly." (Hospitality & Catering News)
- "Bringing the same standards I'd apply in a formal kitchen to food that's meant to be eaten with your hands and enjoyed without ceremony." (Restaurant Online)
- "I love to do everything in house, all my sauces are made from scratch." (Broadsheet)
