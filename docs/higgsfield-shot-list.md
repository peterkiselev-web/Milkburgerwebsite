# The Griddle: Higgsfield shot list

The cinematic concept plays one burger's journey as you scroll, from raw beef on the steel to the finished burger next to a glass of Champagne. These eight clips make that film.

## Rules for every clip
- **Image-to-video, not text-to-video.** Use a real Milk Burger photo as the reference so the burger on screen matches the burger people get. Best references: the Instagram burger close-up and the press shot of two burgers with Champagne (Milk-Burger-4625).
- **Chain the clips.** Where the model supports a start and end frame, use the last frame of the previous clip as the first frame of the next. The scroll then feels like one continuous shot.
- **Locked-off or slow camera only.** No handheld shake, no fast whip pans, no cuts inside a clip. Scrubbing shaky footage back and forth on scroll looks broken.
- **Keep the subject centred.** The site crops 16:9 to a portrait frame on phones, so keep the action in the middle third.
- **Export:** MP4, 1920x1080, 24fps, 4 to 6 seconds each, highest quality available. Name files `01-drop.mp4`, `02-smash.mp4` and so on.
- **Look:** macro lens, shallow depth of field, brushed stainless steel, warm practical light from above, deep burgundy and black in the background. Glossy, juicy, appetising. Never cartoonish.

**Negative prompt for every clip:** cartoon, illustration, plastic-looking food, raw pink patty centre on a smash burger, extra fingers, distorted hands, text, logos, watermark, camera shake, fast cuts, oversaturated, blurry food.

### 01 · The Drop
> Extreme macro, low angle skimming across a gleaming brushed stainless steel flat-top griddle. A loosely packed ball of deep red aged beef sits in the centre. A thin film of fat shimmers and starts to ripple with heat. Tiny wisps of smoke rise. Background falls away into dark burgundy blur. Locked-off camera, very slow push in. Warm overhead light, glossy highlights on the steel. Cinematic, appetising, 24fps.

### 02 · The Smash
> Same angle and lighting. A heavy stainless steel burger press comes down firmly onto the beef ball and flattens it into a thin, wide patty. Fat spits and sizzles outward, lacy edges form instantly, steam bursts up. Slow motion at the moment of impact. Locked-off camera. Macro, shallow depth of field.

### 03 · The Crust
> Overhead three-quarter angle. A thin smashed patty sizzling on stainless steel, its edges browning into a lacy, crisp crust. A metal spatula slides underneath, scrapes, and flips it to reveal a deep mahogany crust. Grease glistens. Smoke curls. Slow, steady camera drift to the right.

### 04 · The Melt
> Close macro side angle. A square slice of bright orange American cheese is laid over the hot patty and slowly melts, draping over the crispy edges and dripping onto the steel. A second patty is stacked on top and gets another slice. Steam rises through warm light. Locked-off camera, slow motion.

### 05 · The Bun
> Macro, low angle. Two halves of a soft, glossy golden milk bun are placed cut-side down on the stainless steel griddle and toast. Butter bubbles at the edges. Lift one half to reveal an even golden toast. Shallow depth of field, warm light, dark burgundy background.

### 06 · The Build
> Side-on macro, eye level, stainless steel counter. A burger is assembled in one slow, continuous take: toasted bottom bun, a swipe of creamy sauce, two smashed patties with dripping melted orange cheese, thick-cut pickle slices, the glossy top bun pressed down gently so cheese oozes out. Locked-off camera. Juicy, glossy, appetising.

### 07 · The Pour
> Close-up of a Champagne glass on a stainless steel counter. Pale gold Champagne is poured in, fine bubbles rising in streams, the foam rising and settling. Background: soft burgundy tile bokeh. Locked-off camera, slow motion, warm light.

### 08 · The Hero
> The finished burger on cream paper on a stainless steel tray, beside a glass of Champagne with rising bubbles and a small pile of crisp golden fries. Slow, smooth push in towards the burger until the glossy bun fills the frame. Warm, intimate light, burgundy tiles softly out of focus behind. Cinematic food commercial, 24fps.

## Optional extras
- **09 · Team Juicy:** the same sequence beats, but a thick patty from aged beef, cut in half to show a juicy centre. Useful for the Smash vs Juicy concept.
- **10 · The Pie:** a golden fried cherry pie broken open over vanilla soft serve, glossy cherry filling oozing out.

## What happens to the clips
The site extracts each clip into a sequence of still frames, which the browser draws onto a canvas as you scroll. This is smoother on iPhones than scrubbing the video directly, and it lets the site load the first frames instantly while the rest stream in. Until the clips arrive, the prototype uses animated stand-ins.
